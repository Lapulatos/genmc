# P0.8b formal decision

## Decision

Reject P0.8b as the generic default and restore the retained `d963c49` production and
test sources. The candidate is exact on all observed executions, but it does not remove
a single complete lazy DFS in the formal corpus, does not establish a CPU improvement,
and adds a persistent-rank RSS risk.

## Formal evidence

- Matrix: 2,304 cells / 1,152 paired cells, four repetitions, 96 tasks,
  SC/TSO/PSO, before/after, 48 BenchExec workers.
- Common-terminal pairs: 1,076; the remaining 76 pairs are matching TIMEOUTs.
- Status, category, terminal verdict and completed-execution mismatches: 0.
- Snapshot-equivalent and current-base mismatches: 0.
- Coverage gains/losses/new OOM: 0/0/0.
- TSO complete-check total: 1,787,856 before and after across 360 counted cells.
- PSO complete-check total: 5,246,664 before and after across 336 counted cells.
- TSO/PSO complete-check and candidate task-model ratios: 1.000000/1.000000.
- Four-repetition task-model CPU geometric-mean ratio: 0.996569.
- Task-model bootstrap 95% CI: [0.992152, 1.000971], which crosses 1.
- SC/TSO/PSO CPU point ratios: 0.997877/0.995420/0.996321.
- RSS cell P90 / P90-of-levels / large-task maximum ratios:
  1.002828/1.000000/1.051310.
- The large-task RSS maximum is PSO `pthread/queue_ok_longest`: 486,318,080 bytes
  before versus 511,270,912 bytes after (+5.13%). Its executions, base/snapshot bytes,
  candidates and 34 complete checks are identical in every repetition.

## Interpretation

Append-stable provisional ranks are correct but do not match the real mutation shape.
Formal TSO/PSO queries either rebuild or introduce an edge that invalidates the cached
order, so validation never replaces a complete DFS. The apparent 0.34% CPU reduction
is not statistically established and has no corresponding work-counter reduction.
Keeping one `uint32_t` rank per stable event/check therefore adds space without reducing
the measured consistency work.

This result rules out the current static-order certificate, not rollback-safe dynamic
topological maintenance in general. A future attempt would need to repair the order
after non-forward insertions and show that repair is cheaper than the exact DFS; it
must not reintroduce this candidate unchanged or use model/task-name selection.
