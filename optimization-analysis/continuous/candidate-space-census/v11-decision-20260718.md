# V11 focus-directed preventive reach decision

## Decision

Reject V11 in its current form. Do not enable `--cat-focus-reach` in the retained
baseline or formal wrappers.

## Correctness evidence

- Analyzer certificate and adversarial focused tests pass.
- GCC 13 Release broad differential: 288 programs x SC/TSO/PSO, 864/864 match,
  zero mismatch and zero unsupported.
- GCC 13 ASan+UBSan: 14/14 focused tests plus an activating CoRR1 execution,
  with no sanitizer diagnostic.
- In the valid complete workload, all common-correct tasks have identical complete
  execution counts and every recorded search counter. Thus the same-certificate
  revision preserves V9's completed candidate search.

## Complete 725-task result

Authoritative input:
`server-results/v11-full-725-20260718b`.

- 725 tasks per variant, 1,450 rows, both BenchExec runs exit 0.
- V11 activates on 216 tasks and records 571,899/571,899 successful focus queries,
  with zero fallback.
- V9 performs 1,205,405 full-root checks and emits 2,943,514,438 root candidates.
  V11 removes those checks and emits 489,083,393 focus-reach candidates.
- CPU V11/V9: 1.108462, bootstrap 95% CI [1.067238, 1.154924].
- Wall V11/V9: 1.111303 [1.069816, 1.156717].
- RSS V11/V9: 0.999895 [0.999670, 1.000116].
- Eighteen V9-completed tasks become V11 TIMEOUT/OOM; there are zero reverse
  coverage transitions.
- On 355 common-correct tasks, the geometric mean `sync-ns` ratio is about 1.2945.

V11 therefore fails both the CPU upper-bound and no-coverage-regression retain gates.

## Excluded run

`v11-full-725-20260718a` is not final evidence. Its experimental selector preferred a
different preventive certificate under V11. That changed RF/CO offered/pruned totals
and produced 31 common-correct search-counter differences, violating the frozen
same-candidate-set protocol even though completed verdicts agreed. The selector change
was removed before the valid B run.

## Measured next direction

The remaining candidate is V11.1: make directional predecessor/successor enumeration
cost proportional to emitted structural edges. Current `Relation::nextPredecessor()`
falls back to a universe scan for ProgramOrder/Internal/External/Location relations,
and positive-root intersection selection does not prefer the sparser materialized base.
Any V11.1 prototype must retain the same V9 certificate and pass the same 864 and
725-task gates. A 96-task matrix is not an effectiveness gate.
